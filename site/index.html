Settings

Appearance

System follows your device's Light or Dark appearance automatically, even while Ghost Beads is open.

Voice

On iPhone and iPad, Ghost Beads uses the offline voice pack only.

Desktop uses the original browser voice by default (Google US English when available). You can switch to the natural offline voice pack. iPhone and iPad always use the voice pack.

Voice can be switched on or off separately for each mode. With voice off, numbers are shown on screen before advancing.

If sound is missing, check your media volume and Bluetooth/AirPlay output. On iOS, the offline voice pack is used automatically.

Exercise settings

Quick flick launches beads almost immediately, reaches travel speed in the first few frames, and stops sharply on contact. The remainder of each move is a still hold. Soft glide retains the older gentler transition.

Smaller pauses separate parts of one exchange; longer pauses separate operand digits. Movement detail changes immediately, including during a spoken operation. Moves already underway finish normally.

Test mode shows only the ghost-bead outlines and the announced numbers. It never animates or lights beads, or shows the running total.

Install & offline

Checking offline availability…

On iPhone or iPad, open the HTTPS website in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, enable “Open as Web App”, then tap Add. Open the home-screen icon once while online to save the app for offline use.

Your exercises and progress are saved on this device. The voice pack works offline after its audio files have been cached. The original browser voice is available on desktops and depends on your installed voices.
